# Dadi's Map: "A Gift for Each Other"
### A day-in-the-life storyboard script (paper + app)

**Cast**
- **Dadi (Kamala, 68).** Draws slowly and carefully, likes pencil. Her ink on screen is **blue**.
- **Aarav (7).** Draws fast and messy, likes crayons. His ink on screen is **orange**.

**Setting.** Dadi's dining table, after school. An iPad stands upright at the far edge of the table, with a cream **tray** (or just a clear patch of table) in front of it. On the table: a stack of printed **drawing sheets** (A4, four grey corner squares, a thin frame), pencils, a crayon box, an eraser, two cups of chai.

**How to read each frame**
- **DRAW:** what goes in the frame
- **SCREEN:** what the iPad shows (exact app copy in quotes)
- **VOICE:** the app's spoken voice (it reads every screen aloud)
- **SAY:** what Dadi and Aarav say
- **SHOWS:** the feature this frame demonstrates

---

## ACT 1: THE FIRST EVENING (setting up)

**F1. The table**
- DRAW: Wide shot. Aarav runs in with his school bag. Dadi is already sitting, with the iPad, the sheets and the crayons laid out. Steam from the chai.
- SAY: Aarav: "Dadi, what's that?" / Dadi: "Your mother put a game on this for us."
- SHOWS: The setting. It's a tabletop ritual, and the paper is real.

**F2. Language**
- DRAW: Close on the screen. Dadi's finger taps **हिन्दी**.
- SCREEN: "Which language are you comfortable in?" with English and हिन्दी.
- VOICE: "Namaste! Which language are you comfortable in?…"
- SAY: Dadi: "Hindi, beta." / Aarav: "Okay, but I can read English too!"
- SHOWS: Hindi/English throughout, with voice in both. *(In later frames you can draw the screen in Hindi, or keep it English for readability.)*

**F3. Aarav says his name**
- DRAW: Aarav leans into the iPad and holds the mic button. A small speech bubble: "AARAV!"
- SCREEN: "Hi! What's your name?" The input shows "Listening…", then fills with **Aarav** in orange.
- SHOWS: Name by voice (or typed). The grandchild starts the onboarding.

**F4. What Aarav loves**
- DRAW: A grid of blob tiles. Aarav slaps **Animals**, **Snow**, **Food**. Each one tilts and grows a little.
- SCREEN: "Aarav, what do you love?"
- SHOWS: Pick-what-you-love tiles. These shape the prompts later.

**F5. Who are you playing with?**
- DRAW: Aarav points at Dadi, then taps **Grandmother**.
- SCREEN: "Aarav, who are you playing with?" Grandmother / Grandfather.
- SHOWS: The app works with any grown-up, not just Dadi.

**F6. Dadi's turn**
- DRAW: Dadi puts on her glasses and types "Kamala" slowly with one finger. Then she picks **Garden**, **God** and **Stories**. A small dot sits on Aarav's Animals tile.
- SCREEN: "Dadi, what do you love?" (the dot marks Aarav's picks)
- SAY: Aarav: "Pick Animals too, Dadi!" / Dadi: "I'll pick my garden."
- SHOWS: Both people's picks, side by side. The prompts are made from both.

**F7. Drawing each other (Dadi draws Aarav)**
- DRAW: Dadi draws on the screen with her finger: a big round head, spiky hair, a huge grin. Aarav laughs.
- SCREEN: "Kamala, draw Aarav!" "Make it silly. Little Aarav will walk around all your maps." Pencil / Eraser / Start over.
- SHOWS: Finger-drawn caricatures.

**F8. Aarav draws Dadi**
- DRAW: Aarav gives Dadi giant round glasses and a bun. He uses the eraser once, then "Start over", then draws her again.
- SAY: Dadi: "My nose is not that big!" / Aarav: "It's *silly*, Dadi. It says so."
- SHOWS: The caricatures become the little paper walkers on the maps later (pay this off in F28).

**F9. How it works**
- DRAW: Three tilted cards on screen, both of them leaning in to read.
- SCREEN: "Daily prompt, together" · "Your drawings come alive" · "Finish maps, walk around!" Button: "Let's draw".
- SHOWS: A three-card explainer, read aloud.

## ACT 2: DAY 1 (the core loop)

**F10. Today's prompt**
- DRAW: A teal sky, a sun, clouds. Big text in the middle. Above it, a small picture of two people drawing on separate sheets.
- SCREEN: **"Draw a gift for each other"**. Button: "We're done!"
- VOICE: "Today's doodle: Draw a gift for each other. Draw it on paper, each on your own sheet…"
- SHOWS: One prompt a day. *(The first map, Welcome Party, already has its first pairs filled in, so this is the last prompt that finishes it.)*

**F11. Drawing on paper (the hero frame for the paper)**
- DRAW: Over-the-shoulder, two sheets side by side. Dadi draws a careful **pencil kite** with a long tail. Aarav draws a **crayon cat** for Dadi, coloured way outside the lines. The corner squares are clearly visible on both sheets.
- SAY: Aarav (whispering): "Don't look!" / Dadi covers her sheet with her hand.
- SHOWS: The paper is the main event, and the screen is off to one side. The corner squares are what let the scanner find the sheet.

**F12. "We're done!"**
- DRAW: Aarav smacks the big orange button.
- SHOWS: Both draw first, then scan.

**F13. Scanning Dadi's sheet**
- DRAW: Dadi slides her sheet onto the tray. The screen is mustard yellow, with a stamp-shaped viewfinder showing her paper live. Her thumb is resting on one corner.
- SCREEN: "Put **Kamala**'s drawing on the tray" (her name in a blue pill). Status: "Found the paper", then "Hold still…"
- SHOWS: Front-camera tray capture. It finds the sheet by the corner squares, even with a hand over one corner.

**F14. Auto-capture**
- DRAW: A yellow scan bar sweeps down, the drawing appears top to bottom, and a green check pops in.
- SCREEN: "It's scanned!" Button: "Now Aarav's turn".
- SHOWS: It captures by itself once the paper is still for about a second. No buttons needed.

**F15. Aarav's turn (the hands and voice frame)**
- DRAW: Aarav jams his sheet onto the tray crookedly and keeps his hand on it.
- SCREEN: "Hands away from the paper"
- DRAW (next beat): He lifts his hand and shouts "**TAKE!**" (draw it as a big speech bubble). Click.
- SHOWS: Hand detection, and voice capture ("take" / "click" / "cheese").

**F16. "Is it a…?" / "Not quite?"**
- DRAW: Close on the screen. Under Aarav's cat it says "Looks like: **fox**". Aarav is outraged.
- SAY: Aarav: "It's a CAT!"
- DRAW (next beat): He taps "Not quite?", then taps **cat** from the guesses (or types it).
- SCREEN: "What did you draw?" "Tap the right one, and it will move the right way on the map."
- SHOWS: Recognition of any drawing, and the family can correct it. The label decides how it moves.

**F17. Today's sketches**
- DRAW: A pink screen with giant mustard letters "TODAY'S / SKETCHES" behind two bobbing stamps: Dadi's kite (blue name) and Aarav's cat (orange name). Each has a small "Retake" button.
- VOICE: "Here are today's sketches! Tap Show on map to see them come alive."
- SAY: Dadi: "Look, my kite is on a stamp."
- SHOWS: Both drawings, each kept as that person's own. A retake is possible.

**F18. They come alive**
- DRAW: A 3D floating island. Dadi's kite **rises up** out of it, followed by Aarav's cat a moment later. They are puffy paper stickers with white rims, still in their own pencil and crayon lines. The kite flies in circles high up; the cat walks.
- SAY: Aarav: "MY CAT IS WALKING." / Dadi (quietly): "Arre… my kite is flying."
- SHOWS: **The drawing isn't redrawn.** Their own lines, made 3D, moving according to what they are (fly / walk / swim / sway…).

**F19. The place is finished**
- DRAW: The island fills with colour. Earlier gifts, party lights and music notes are all on it.
- SCREEN: "Our place is ready." Then "See what we made". Then "Complete!"
- SHOWS: Prompts finish a place, and finishing reveals it.

**F20. The wait**
- DRAW: The home screen shows a little clock with a sunburst face and a countdown bar. "Day 2". Dadi is clearing the chai cups. Aarav pokes the screen.
- SCREEN: "All done for today" "Next prompt in 14:22:07"
- VOICE: "All done for today! Come back tomorrow for the next doodle."
- SAY: Aarav: "Can't we do one more?" / Dadi: "Tomorrow. That's the fun."
- SHOWS: One a day. No streaks, no pressure, and a missed day just carries the prompt over.

## ACT 3: THE DAYS GO BY (montage, one panel each)

Draw these as a grid of small panels, a calendar-page feel, with the light changing between them.

**F21. Day 3: Living Room**
- Prompt: **"Draw one object in the room that Dadi loves the most."**
- DRAW: Aarav draws Dadi's **radio**. Dadi draws her **prayer lamp (diya)**. They look at each other: they both drew something of hers.
- SHOWS: The prompts are about *each other*. The diya will flicker on the map.

**F22. Day 5: a blurry scan**
- DRAW: Aarav waves the paper at the camera. The screen says "That came out a bit blurry", "Let's try that again".
- SHOWS: Friendly error states (blurry / empty page / "more than a drawing").

**F23. Day 6: Together day**
- DRAW: The home sky has turned warm and orange. One single sheet between them, both drawing on it at once, elbows bumping.
- SCREEN: Small title "Collaborative Task". "Draw the furniture, decorations and things you would want in the living room."
- SHOWS: Together days, with one shared drawing scanned once. The room builds up: "A plant and a picture frame. The living room is ready!"

**F24. Day 8: Kitchen**
- Prompt: **"Draw what grandma uses to make chapattis."**
- DRAW: Dadi draws a perfect **belan** (rolling pin). Aarav draws a **tawa** with a smiley face.
- SAY: Dadi: "Who taught you what a tawa is?" / Aarav: "You did."
- SHOWS: Culture and memory passed across the table.

**F25. Day 9: the Dream House (bonus)**
- SCREEN: "Draw ur dream house." Rule line: "**Colour the door brown. It has to be the darkest colour in your drawing.**"
- DRAW: One sheet. Dadi draws the shape, Aarav adds a slide out of the window, and Dadi carefully colours the door dark brown.
- SCREEN (scan): "Looking for the door…", then "Door found! It's the darkest colour."
- SHOWS: A special scan that reads colour. "We found the door! Your dream house is going up around the rooms."

## ACT 4: WALKING INSIDE (the payoff)

**F26. The map has come alive**
- DRAW: The Dream House map: Living Room, Kitchen, the house and the Park on floating islands. Everything they drew is moving: the diya flickers, the cat walks, the kite circles.
- VOICE: "Your map has come alive! Press Play and follow the light to the red pin."
- SHOWS: Places add up to a whole map, and it opens a new way to play.

**F27. Play**
- DRAW: Aarav grabs the iPad stand. Dadi presses **Play** for him. The view drops to ground level, a joystick appears, and a path of light leads off.
- SCREEN: "Joystick to walk · drag to look around"
- SHOWS: A first-person walk inside your own drawings.

**F28. Meeting themselves**
- DRAW: On screen, the two finger-drawn caricatures from F7 and F8 are walking on the map: Dadi with giant glasses, Aarav with spiky hair. Aarav taps "Dadi". A bubble says "Hi there!" He taps the cat: "Meow!" He taps the chai: "Mmm, chai!"
- SAY: Dadi (laughing): "That's me with my big nose."
- SHOWS: The caricatures are now walkers (they stop to smell flowers and wave at the river), and tapping anything makes it talk.

**F29. The red pin**
- DRAW: Following the light to a red pin. A card pops up.
- SCREEN: "Last prompt" "Build a bridge to go to your next map!" Buttons: "Let's draw it" / "Keep exploring".
- SHOWS: The story leads on. You unlock the next map by walking to it.

**F30. Building the bridge**
- DRAW: Next evening. Both of them drawing a bridge on one sheet, Dadi's careful planks next to Aarav's rainbow railings. On screen: the bridge grows from the pin to a new floating island.
- SCREEN: "A bridge grows from the pin to a floating island. What could be over there?" Then "Cross the bridge" / "On to our next map".
- SHOWS: Map-to-map progression. Next is a themed map like The Hillside, The Animal Sanctuary or The Seaside, picked from what they love.

---

## ACT 5: THE SCRAPBOOK (the keepsake)

**F31. Our scrapbook**
- DRAW: A pink screen, "OUR SCRAPBOOK". A page with three entries. Each one shows the **real paper scans** (Dadi's pencil kite, Aarav's crayon cat, with the paper texture) above a taped paper note: "Day 1 · Draw a gift for each other". Another note says "Day 6 · Together day".
- VOICE: "This is your scrapbook. Swipe, or tap the arrows, to turn the pages."
- SHOWS: Every scan is kept, as the actual paper and not a redraw. "In our scrapbook" also opens straight from any place on the map.

**F32. All our maps**
- DRAW: Zoom out to a stack of maps like cream boards: Welcome Party, Dream House, and a new ghostly half-built one on top.
- SCREEN: "All our maps" "Scroll or drag to look through · tap a map to go inside"
- SHOWS: The archive grows over months.

**F33. Closing frame**
- DRAW: Evening. The iPad is dimmed. On the fridge (drawn in the real world), Aarav's crayon cat is stuck up with a magnet. Dadi tucks the pile of drawing sheets into a folder labelled "Aarav". Aarav at the door: "Same time tomorrow, Dadi?"
- TEXT ON FRAME: **Dadi's Map: one prompt, two drawings, every day.**
